Frequently asked questions.

Answers to common questions about living at Nexus.

Package inclusions vary, but options can include utilities, Wi-Fi, cleaning, co-working access and shared facilities.
Send an enquiry through the Contact page. Our team can discuss your dates, preferred package and requirements.
Short-Term Stay is designed for flexible 1–4 week stays. Longer packages can be arranged according to availability.
Pet policies depend on the residence and room type. Contact the team before booking.
Cancellation terms depend on the package and booking agreement. Please ask for the current terms when enquiring.
Accessibility requirements can be discussed through a Custom Package enquiry.
Nexus is designed around convenient Singapore city living. The team can provide route guidance for your selected residence.
Changes depend on availability and your agreement. Contact us and we can discuss suitable options.
